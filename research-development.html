Research & Development

We investigate how people, organizations and technology shape the experience and future of work, then develop evidence and ideas that can support better decisions.

Research & Development Products Education & Knowledge Strategy & Solutions
Working research agenda

Five connected areas of inquiry.

These starting areas describe the current scope. Their names and boundaries can be refined as the research agenda develops.

People Analytics & Workforce Intelligence

Use workforce evidence to understand patterns and inform decisions about people and work.

Organizational Psychology

Study how people experience work and how organizational conditions shape behavior and well-being.

Talent

Explore how organizations recognize, support and develop talent.

Skills & Capability Development

Understand how skills and organizational capabilities grow and adapt.

Technology Adoption & Impact

Examine how people and organizations adopt technology and what changes as a result.

From inquiry to application

Build understanding. Develop what works.

Research starts with meaningful questions from organizations and society. Evidence helps us understand those questions, test ideas and find practical ways to apply what we learn.

Ask better questions

Frame relevant questions about people, work, organizational life and technological change.

Develop evidence and ideas

Use appropriate research and analytical methods to build understanding, test approaches and develop frameworks or prototypes.

Translate learning

Share findings in forms that can inform practice, education and further development of solutions.
