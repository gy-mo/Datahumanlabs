About Data Human Labs

Human questions. Evidence-led work.

Data Human Labs brings together research, education and applied work at the intersection of people, organizations and technology. Our purpose is to support people’s well-being and potential while helping organizations make sound, evidence-informed decisions.

We build knowledge over time, share it openly where useful, and work with organizations to translate evidence into strategies and solutions.
